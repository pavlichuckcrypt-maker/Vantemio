VANTEMIO — separate accounts and concurrent sessions

Accounts and sessions are separate. Wallet proof binds origin, address, chain, expiry and a single-use challenge. Account context is explicit. Inventory, asset and order access is owner-scoped. Logout or wallet changes invalidate queued work rather than silently switching actors.

The source includes persistent user/account storage, PostgreSQL and SQLite adapters, concurrent-session controls, admission queues, request limits and recovery of fixed transaction intents. SQLite creates a new local database by default; original-machine user databases are excluded from GitHub. The presence of a PostgreSQL adapter does not mean an external production database has been configured.

Fresh local tests use disposable data and simulated identities. They do not supply real credentials or establish public production capacity. Legacy filenames are retained for interface compatibility; this document is in English.
