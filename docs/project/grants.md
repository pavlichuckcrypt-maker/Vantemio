# Grant project: repeatable Vantemio Studio production

0.17 · 2026-10-08

Vantemio seeks resources to develop a production engine for creators and teams. The funded stage connects scripting, graphics, voice, editing and review tools into a controlled workflow with project memory, local executors and targeted revisions.

## Problem and technical outcome

Creators manually move context between services, repeat instructions and rebuild work after changes. Studio must retain project state, provide the assistant with relevant context, execute bounded operations and preserve accepted parts. The deliverable is a reproducible path from an agreed brief to reviewed video and a corrected revision.

## Work packages

| Package | Deliverable | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Context and execution | Versioned scenes, inputs, tasks and results | Resume interruption without replaying completed work |
| Editing, graphics and audio | Connected creative plan and assembly | Brief compliance, valid file and agreed artistic direction |
| Review and revisions | Defect evidence and a new version | Repair affected parts and recheck output and dependencies |
| Local and cloud adapters | Resource- and permission-based execution | Common task contract, data-transfer controls and reproducibility |
| Measurement and documentation | Comparable test and results package | Tokens, human time, rendering, quality and all attempts recorded |

## Stages and responsibility

The internal prototype is the first stage of the investment plan. Briefs, quality criteria, deliverables, period and owners are fixed before work. The founder leads product architecture; the planned team covers core/AI integrations and product/cloud/APIs. Limited user pilots and delivery preparation follow. Official Vantemio channels provide a testing and demonstration environment.

## Method and review material

Comparisons use the same source materials, task and quality criteria. Cloud-assistant calls/tokens, active human time, elapsed time, rendering and costs are measured separately. Failed attempts and human interventions remain in the record. Tests assess context retention, reuse of permitted techniques and targeted rework. Evidence includes briefs, versions, output, QA, recovery records and calculation methods.

## Budget and application scope

The 12-month plan is **US$150,000** for solo development: US$100,000 for development, resources, marketing and equipment plus US$50,000 founder compensation. The alternative **US$400,000** scenario includes that same US$150,000, two developers at US$240,000 annually and a US$10,000 additional reserve. Developer compensation is calculated as 2 × US$10,000 × 12 months.

DGX Spark with 128 GB shared CPU/GPU memory and a 4 TB SSD has a US$6,950 allowance before tax and shipping within the base US$100,000. Each programme receives defined eligible work packages, expenses, applicant, territory, period, matching funds and reporting. Cash and equipment are not counted twice. [Full investment plan](investors.md). Customer pricing is determined separately.

## Separate Base/Boson direction

The commerce module forms a separate project: order terms → output version → quality control → delivery → acceptance → settlement or refund. Tests cover buyer rights, signatures, hashes, deadlines, retries and transaction outcomes. A full multi-provider marketplace and physical goods follow in later stages.
