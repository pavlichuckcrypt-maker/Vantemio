# Vantemio Privacy Policy

Revision 0.16 · 8 October 2026

## 1. Operator and scope

Vitalii Pavlichuk is responsible for Vantemio and personal information, operating as an individual in Alberta, Canada. For processing, access, correction, deletion and overseas-provider enquiries: [support@vantemio.com](mailto:support@vantemio.com), subject “Privacy”.

This policy covers vantemio.com, account sign-in, correspondence, applications and processing for Vantemio official channels. Authentication data and retention are described in the account sign-in section. Connected product features use the purposes and separate user authorizations described below.

The operator applies Alberta PIPA to processing within its scope and PIPEDA to applicable commercial interprovincial and international personal-information flows. Mandatory user rights under applicable law remain protected. [PIPA](https://www.alberta.ca/personal-information-protection-act) · [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/).

## 3. Bases and separate permissions

Information is processed for disclosed purposes with required consent or another applicable lawful basis. Collection is limited to what is necessary. An ordinary application does not authorize marketing, model training, account connection, publication or spending funds.

Application information is not sold or used by Vantemio to train models. Production materials go to external AI providers only as necessary for the agreed scenario and subject to their terms. New purposes are disclosed before related processing.

## 4. Providers and international processing

Cloudflare provides website delivery and protection, D1 stores applications and Turnstile protects the form. The mail provider processes correspondence. Publishing platforms receive authorized material and necessary metadata. Providers do not receive unrestricted usage rights at Vantemio's discretion; their roles and own terms are disclosed for the relevant feature.

Provider infrastructure may be outside Canada. Before using an overseas provider, the operator identifies and discloses applicable countries, purposes and processing safeguards in the current provider list. That list and overseas-processing practices are available from the responsible operator on request. The operator's location does not imply Canada-only storage.

Information may be disclosed to meet a binding legal requirement. Hashed IP data is treated as protected technical information, not guaranteed anonymous data. Further information: [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 5. Retention

Applications are retained for **no more than 90 calendar days from receipt**, then deleted or irreversibly anonymized. Vantemio undertakes to provide scheduled cleanup independently of website traffic. Continued consideration does not automatically extend retention: any subsequent separate service establishes its purposes, terms and retention.

| Category | Maximum duration / event |
|---|---|
| Application, text and confirmation | 90 days from receipt |
| Ordinary enquiry correspondence | 90 days from the last substantive message |
| Website technical logs | 30 days, excluding a separately recorded incident |
| Rate-limit and duplicate-request records | 24 hours from creation |
| Browser technical identity | 24 hours from creation or earlier user removal |
| Disconnected-account tokens | Use stops on disconnection; deletion within 7 days or a shorter platform deadline |
| API analytics data | Refresh or delete at least every 30 days; shorter applicable revocation rules take priority |
| Backups | Do not extend the relevant category's maximum retention |

These periods govern Vantemio-controlled information; independent platforms handle received information under their own policies. A published video is not automatically removed after 90 days with an application.

Where specific records are needed for a contract, mandatory accounting, legal claims or incident investigation, only necessary information is retained on that basis. The operator records the purpose and separate duration; records are not reused for unrelated purposes. Contract-production retention is disclosed before accepting the corresponding materials.

## 6. Requests and rights

Individuals may request processing information, access, a copy, correction, deletion or consent withdrawal, subject to applicable legal limits. Write from the application email and include its number if available. Identity verification must be proportionate; do not send a passport without a separately justified request.

Requests are handled within applicable statutory periods. A refusal or necessary retention is explained. Concerns may be referred to [OIPC Alberta](https://oipc.ab.ca/) or, within its jurisdiction, the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/).

## 7. YouTube and future social connections

The official YouTube channel uses permissions necessary for connected publishing and analysis features. The [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and YouTube rules apply. Google access may be revoked in [permission settings](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions); contact the operator about Vantemio-held data. Revoking access and removing an existing publication are separate actions.

TikTok connection requires disclosure of permissions, data categories and recipients before authorization. OAuth establishes the connection; posting requires the prescribed creator confirmation.

YouTube deletion covers all associated user API data, not only tokens. A deletion request, connection removal or consent revocation through Vantemio triggers deletion without undue delay and within 7 calendar days; token revocation is communicated to Google immediately. For revocation through Google settings, Vantemio also adopts a stricter internal maximum of 7 days from revocation, with regular authorization checks. This internal standard does not replace the distinct deadlines in [YouTube API policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies). The general 30-day target does not apply to these records.

## 8. Cookies and safeguards

The website does not use advertising trackers. Necessary Cloudflare mechanisms and browser technical storage support security and request processing. Additional analytics receives advance disclosure and any required choice.

Access to personal information is limited to necessary authority. Credentials are protected and excluded from public logs. In an incident, the operator assesses risk, limits harm and meets applicable regulator and individual notification obligations.

The website also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for visit and page-performance statistics. Cloudflare adds its beacon to public HTML. The provider states that Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data; Vantemio does not use these metrics for advertising personalization. This is separate from network security and application processing. [Web Analytics overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/) · [Metric collection](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/data-metrics/data-origin-and-collection/).

The website policy’s 90-day period applies to applications and its specified categories, not automatic deletion of projects on the user’s computer. Home users manage local projects and backups. Activation, diagnostics and support data, recipients and retention are disclosed separately before release. Sending source media or project history for support requires a separate clear user action; credentials and passwords are excluded from that export.

## 10. Future TikTok-connector data

This section defines processing when the TikTok connector is connected. Connection processes account identity, permissions and tokens; transfer processes selected video, caption, settings, creator confirmation, `publish_id` and status. Necessary information goes to TikTok and storage/delivery providers within the operation. Advertising and model training need a separate basis.

A temporary copy without confirmed transfer authorization is deleted within 24 hours of creation. Delivery copies are deleted within 24 hours after cancellation or completed transfer. For every started transfer, the delivery-copy lifetime is capped at 7 days from the first attempt. If no final outcome is established by then, the operation closes as unresolved; the copy is then deleted, the operator notified and automatic retry remains prohibited. A minimal authorization/outcome record is retained for up to 90 days after closing the operation, including an unresolved outcome, unless shorter platform rules require earlier deletion. The source Studio project has a separate agreed retention period and is not a temporary delivery copy.

Token refresh does not extend consent to new purposes. Revocation stops new actions and tokens follow section 5 deletion deadlines. The [connector description](tiktok.md) and [deletion procedure](data-deletion.md) are accessible without login. TikTok follows its own [policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/row/privacy-policy/en).

## Account sign-in

When account sign-in is available, Google authentication uses your Google account identifier to recognise your account; the server checks the identity token and email verification status. We do not request access to Gmail, Drive or YouTube for sign-in. Wallet sign-in records the public wallet address and verifies a one-time message. We never request your recovery phrase or private key. Authentication data is not used to train content-generation models.

A secure, HttpOnly session cookie lasts up to 12 hours; the server stores a hash of its random token. Sign-in challenges expire after five minutes and can be used once. Expired challenges and sessions are removed during subsequent authentication activity. Account identity records remain until deletion is requested. Signing out revokes the current session. Deleting an account removes its sign-in identity and all its sessions; separate applications and immutable blockchain records are handled as described in the data-deletion policy.

To prevent repeated unsuccessful sign-ins, we keep a keyed hash of the source IP and a consecutive-failure counter. Four failed identity checks in a row block new sign-ins for four hours. A successful sign-in clears the counter. The sign-in database does not store the raw IP for this purpose. Expired block records are removed on the next check; earlier failures remain until a successful sign-in or a completed block.
